Session One: An Introduction for SLS Staff

The first of the library’s staff AI trainings: what generative AI is, what it is useful for, and how to approach it problem-first rather than tool-first. The presentation is below.

Presented
Tuesday, 24 June 2025
Facilitators
Taryn Marks and Will Huggins, Robert Crown Law Library
Written for
Stanford Law School staff, with no prior AI experience assumed

From June 2025, and the earliest material in this archive. The framing holds; the specific tools and screenshots are two generations old by now. For a current introduction, read Introduction to AI for Beginners.
