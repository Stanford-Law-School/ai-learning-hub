The AI Upload

The Robert Crown Law Library’s weekly digest of artificial intelligence news for the Stanford Law School community — the tools, the court and policy developments, and the practical guidance worth your attention, gathered into one short read.

New every Friday

The digest opens in a new tab on its own address. No sign-in is needed — it is open to the whole SLS community, and to anyone else who finds it.

What is in each issue

  • The week’s AI news, selected by hand rather than generated — articles are searched and chosen by people at the Library.
  • A podcast edition of each issue, for reading time you do not have.
  • Pro tips — a short, practical technique each week, from AI in Gmail to context engineering.
  • An archive of past issues and tips, so a week you missed is still there.
  • An AI transparency statement naming the tools used to produce it.

New issues every Friday, minus Stanford holidays and closures. Questions or suggestions? Email library@law.stanford.edu.
