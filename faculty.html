AI and Technology Support for SLS Faculty

The Robert Crown Law Library runs a separate site of AI and technology support written for Stanford Law School faculty and staff — teaching guides, ready-made skill files, and step-by-step tutorials. It sits behind Stanford sign-in, on its own address.

Go to the faculty site

Stanford sign-in

The faculty site opens in a new tab and asks you to sign in with your Stanford Google account. It is a separate site with its own address, so signing in there does not sign you in here, and nothing you do here is carried across.

Access Faculty Resources↗ (opens in a new tab)

What is on the faculty site

  • Teaching guides. Short, judgment-first guides on bringing AI into a course, responding to suspected student AI use, and reading an AI answer critically.
  • Skill files for ChatGPT and Claude. Course and syllabus design, assessment and rubric building, Socratic question design, reading-list curation, and research support.
  • Tutorial library. Step-by-step documents to download and work through.
  • AI resources and EdTech. Which tools are available at Stanford, what each is good for, and the policy that governs them.

Who can sign in

  • SLS faculty and staff only, with a Stanford Google account at @stanford.edu or @law.stanford.edu. Personal Google accounts are not accepted.
  • Faculty and staff on the Law Library’s list are approved already — sign in and the site opens.
  • Anyone else with an eligible Stanford address can request access from the sign-in page, and an administrator reviews it.

Trouble signing in? Email library@law.stanford.edu and the Law Library will confirm your access another way.

Are you a student?

The faculty site is for faculty and staff. Everything written for students — the AI skills, the tutorials, the reading lists, and the workshops — is here on the AI Learning Hub, and you do not need an approval to use any of it.
